# Gym

A workout app for the iPhone home screen: plans, about 80 exercises with tips, every set logged with its weight and reps, a rest timer, and your progress. Everything is saved on the phone and it works offline.

Open https://levon-z.github.io/gym-app/ in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".

The look is inspired by the FORM strength training app concept by Sujon Hossain. Font: Urbanist (SIL Open Font License, see fonts/OFL.txt).
